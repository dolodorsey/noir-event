# NOIR Intake Authority

NOIR public forms are owned by this repository and submit through the established NOIR n8n workflow.

Primary forms:
- `/forms/rsvp`
- `/forms/birthday`
- `/forms/vendor`

Supporting forms:
- `/forms/table_reservation`
- `/forms/sponsor`
- `/forms/influencer`
- `/forms/volunteer`
- `/forms/artist_music`

KHG Forms enterprise URLs redirect into these canonical routes.
